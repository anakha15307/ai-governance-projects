# Sources

All sources are public web pages accessed September 2026. Claims in
[ASSESSMENT.md](./ASSESSMENT.md) are based on these; vendor marketing claims
are reported as vendor-stated.

## Product description and features

- https://www.edugenius.app/blog/khanmigo-vs-socratic-by-google-which-is-better-for-teachers
  — Socratic tutoring design, teacher tools (lesson plans, rubrics, IEP
  progress notes), teacher dashboard with student transcripts.
- https://smartstaffroom.com/khanmigo-review/
  — Dual tutor/assistant functions, GPT-4 basis, guided-questioning design,
  pricing ($4/mo), FERPA & COPPA claims.
- https://moge.ai/product/khanmigo
  — Feature list: interactive tutoring, teacher tools, safety/privacy focus,
  Canvas integration, subject coverage.
- https://opentools.ai/tools/khanmigo-by-khan-academy
  — Teacher/learner/parent/district use cases; pricing tiers (teacher free,
  $4/mo individual/family, district quoted).
- http://topai.tools/t/khan-academy-khanmigo
  — Under-18 parent activation, teacher district implementation, plan
  pricing, child accounts.

## Privacy and safety posture

- https://teachtools.co/blog/secure-edtech-tools-k12-privacy-ferpa-coppa
  — No training-data use (vendor-stated), anonymized OpenAI transfers,
  moderation flagging with adult notification; pricing summary.
- https://mastersineducation.org/articles/ai-in-the-classroom-student-privacy-2026-08-30/
  — Khanmigo Lite data-use caveat; FERPA/COPPA teacher guidance.
- https://medium.com/@momsync01/i-audited-khanmigo-against-5-child-ai-safety-standards-heres-what-i-found-099943d531dd
  — Independent commentary audit: hallucination acknowledgment, no
  systematic uncertainty signal, parental consent, conversation-history
  visibility. (Opinion/commentary, not a formal audit.)
- https://github.com/ombharatiya/ai-tools/blob/HEAD/professions/teacher.md
  — COPPA rule changes (2025/2026), "FERPA aligned" marketing vs. signed
  agreement caution.

## Limitations, efficacy, criticism

- https://www.techlearning.com/news/what-is-khanmigo-the-gpt-4-learning-tool-explained-by-sal-khan
  — Sal Khan on hallucination/math errors being rare but real; pricing and
  district signup details.
- https://clio.fit/the-students-did-not-get-answers-from-khanmigo-they-didnt-want-his-questions-either-clio/
  — Tennessee RCT coverage: no measurable gains beyond Khan Academy alone,
  low engagement; Khan's response and product changes since 2023.
- https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/rip-khanmigo-edtech-industry-dreams-ai-tutors-dan-meyer-bfuec
  — Opinion piece critiquing efficacy evidence and user projections.
  (Commentary, not empirical research.)
- https://community.oerproject.com/cfs-file/__key/communityserver-wikis-components-files/00-00-00-00-02/OER-Project-_2D00_-Khanmigo-Guide.pdf
  — OER Project guide: content-integration errors, task-execution issues,
  prompt-engineering challenges.
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40455514
  — Hacker News discussion of the 2024 Microsoft/Khan Academy partnership
  (free US teacher tools, Azure backing, Phi-3 math-tutor collaboration
  using Khan Academy math data, not user data).

## Notes on source quality

- Review/directory sites (edugenius, smartstaffroom, opentools, topai,
  moge) are secondary sources; feature and pricing details should be
  verified against Khan Academy's own documentation before real use.
- The Medium "audit" and LinkedIn essay are opinion/commentary, cited as
  such — not treated as empirical findings.
- No primary-source (khanacademy.org) pages were fetched in this pass;
  vendor claims marked "vendor-stated" await DPA-level verification.

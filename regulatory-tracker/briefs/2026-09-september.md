## 1. EU AI Act implementation

### What changed this month (illustrative)

- Implementing guidance continues to evolve around high-risk system
  obligations (risk management, data governance, technical documentation,
  logging, human oversight). Deployers should track AI Office publications
  rather than relying on summaries.
- General-purpose AI model obligations (documentation, transparency, and —
  for models with systemic risk — evaluation and incident reporting) remain
  the active compliance frontier for model providers.

### Upcoming obligations and dates (illustrative — verify)

| Obligation | Who it affects | Illustrative date — verify |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk AI system requirements apply | Providers/deployers of Annex III high-risk systems | Phased — verify current date |
| Transparency obligations for certain AI systems | Providers and deployers of listed systems | Phased — verify current date |
| General-purpose AI obligations | Providers of GPAI models | In force — verify scope |

### What it means for us (illustrative)

- Any use case classified high-risk (see the
  [edtech risk assessment](../edtech-risk-assessment/)) needs its
  documentation, logging, and oversight evidence assembled ahead of the
  applicable date — not after.
- Vendor contracts should allocate provider vs. deployer responsibilities
  explicitly (see the [vendor checklist](../vendor-ai-assessment/)).
